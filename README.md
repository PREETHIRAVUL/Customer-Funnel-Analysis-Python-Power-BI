# Customer Funnel Analysis

## Business Problem:
The business experienced low conversion rates due to major customer drop-offs across the purchase funnel, particularly between browsing and checkout stages. Limited visibility into funnel performance across channels, regions, and product categories made it difficult to identify revenue leakage and optimize conversion strategies.

---

## Objective
Analyze customer behavior across the funnel journey to identify high drop-off stages, evaluate conversion performance by channel and region, and uncover opportunities to improve revenue conversion.

## Key Insights
- Highest drop-off occurred at the **Purchase stage (69.35%)**
- Organic traffic delivered the highest conversion rate at **11.19%**
- South region achieved the strongest conversion performance at **11.25%**
- Beauty category recorded the highest product conversion rate at **11.43%**
- Cart abandonment accounted for **50.08%** of potential lost customers
- Identified an estimated **$3.84M revenue recovery opportunity** through funnel optimization

---

## Business Recommendations
- Optimize checkout experience to reduce purchase-stage abandonment
- Increase focus on high-performing organic acquisition channels
- Replicate successful regional strategies in underperforming markets
- Promote high-converting product categories to maximize sales efficiency
- Implement cart abandonment recovery campaigns and retargeting strategies
